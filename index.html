A Little Note For You

My dearest,

To the most amazing girl in my life, Happy Girlfriend's Day, my love. ❤️ I don't even know where to begin because no matter how many words I write, they'll never be enough to explain what you mean to me. Thank you for always being there for me. For listening to me when I overthink, for making me laugh when I need it the most, for believing in me even when I don't believe in myself. You somehow make my bad days feel lighter and my good days feel even brighter. Your support isn't just something you do—it's something I feel every single day, and I'll never take it for granted. One of my favorite memories will always be the night we stayed on call until the morning. We were probably tired, saying random things, laughing over the smallest moments, and forgetting that time even existed. It wasn't anything grand, but somehow it became one of the happiest memories I have. I realized that as long as I was with you, even doing absolutely nothing felt like the best part of my day. I know we won't always have perfect days. We'll have busy schedules, misunderstandings, and moments where life gets difficult. But if there's one thing I want you to know, it's this: I'll always choose us. Every single time. You make my heart feel at home. You're the person I want to tell everything to—the first person I think about when something good happens and the one I want beside me when things aren't going well. That's something really special, and it's all because of you. I hope you never forget how beautiful your heart is. Your kindness, your smile, your patience, your little habits, the way you care about people, and even the tiny things you don't notice about yourself—they're all reasons I fall for you a little more every day. And yes... thank you for putting up with my dumb jokes, my random moods, and my dramatic moments. I know I can be a little chaotic sometimes, but somehow you still choose me. I promise I'll keep finding new ways to make you smile, annoy you just enough, and remind you every day how loved you are. If I had one wish, it would be for us to make thousands more memories together. More late-night calls that accidentally turn into sunrise conversations. More laughs that make our stomachs hurt. More little moments that seem ordinary but become unforgettable because we shared them together. So today, on Girlfriend's Day, I just want to say thank you. Thank you for loving me. Thank you for trusting me. Thank you for supporting me. Thank you for being you. No matter what happens, a part of my heart will always belong to you. I love you more than words could ever explain. Happy Girlfriend's Day, my pretty girl. ❤️ Forever yours, ❤️

You are my favorite person, my safe place, my greatest adventure, and my forever home. Thank you for being exactly who you are.

Happy Girlfriend's Day, my love. Here's to us — today, tomorrow, and always.

With all my heart,
Your Boyfriend 💜
